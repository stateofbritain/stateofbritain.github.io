Public Appointments Board

Open chair, non-executive and board member roles on UK public bodies, collected from the Cabinet Office public appointments service and NHS England. Most are part-time, fixed-term ministerial appointments.

Closing Dates

Each mark is one advert, placed on its application deadline. Larger marks advertise more than one seat. Select a mark to jump to the role.

Roles

    Sources

    Cabinet Office, Apply for a public appointment. Covers appointments made by UK government ministers. Role type and regulated status come from the service's own search filters.

    NHS England, Non-executive opportunities. Chairs and non-executive directors of NHS trusts and foundation trusts.

    Derived figures

    Annual pay is estimated from the advertised rate. Ranges use the midpoint and day rates are multiplied by the advertised days per year. Weekly commitments assume 46 working weeks. Per-meeting rates are not annualised.

    Day rate is annual pay divided by days per year. Advertised figures should be checked against the full advert.

    Coverage

    NHS adverts usually set out pay and term in the candidate pack rather than the advert text, so these fields are blank. Scottish, Welsh and Northern Ireland appointments are advertised separately and are not yet included. Some senior roles are advertised only through recruitment firms.

    "Recently closed" shows Cabinet Office adverts whose deadline fell in the 60 days before collection.
